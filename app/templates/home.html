Spotify Readme Widgets

Live Spotify SVG widgets for embedding in a GitHub README.

Endpoints

PathShows
/apiCurrently playing (or most recent) track, with a progress bar
/api/topTop 5 tracks over the last ~4 weeks
/api/recentLast 5 played tracks

Customization

ParamEffect
themelight (default) or dark
bgBackground hex color, e.g. bg=1a1a1a
barsNumber of EQ bars, 4-20 (/api only)

Embed

<img src="{{ base_url }}api" alt="Current Spotify Song">
Source & setup instructions: github.com/adehikmatfr/spotify-readme
